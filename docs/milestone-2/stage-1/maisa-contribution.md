# Maisa — Stage 1 Contributions

## Overview
For Maharti's Milestone 2 — Stage 1, Maisa prepared two mobile interface designs: **Notifications** and **Session Scheduling**.

## 1. Notifications Screen
The supplied design shows:
- A heading **Notifications** with the subtitle *Updates from your skill exchanges*.
- A notification that **Layan accepted your exchange request**.
- A reminder that a **Logo Design session starts tomorrow**.
- A notification that **Omar left you a review**.
- Distinct pastel notification cards with icons and relative timestamps.

**Design goal:** Make exchange-related updates visible and easy to scan.

## 2. Session Scheduling Screen
The supplied design shows:
- **Schedule Session** heading and a skill exchange labeled *Logo Design ↔ PowerPoint*.
- A date-selection area with weekday chips and an October 2026 calendar.
- Available time slots **4:00 PM**, **5:30 PM**, and **7:00 PM**.
- A prominent **Confirm Session** button.

**Design goal:** Help students select a date and time for a planned skill exchange.
